## Context

A native CE census (ce-probe-001) showed that Land_Tenement_Small and Land_Tenement_Big have 0 loot points and lootmax 0, both in the stock files and in the engine's own CEApi proxy export. The census also found the buildings empty in game. Barracks are filled up to their lootmax.

The fix therefore has to author lootFloor points at positions validated in the engine. The pipeline has three steps:

1. `tools/setup/interior_sampler.py` stages a server-only init.c hook. The hook does the following:
   - casts vertical DayZPhysics.RaycastRV rays over a model-space grid inside each sampled building;
   - keeps only floor hits that belong to the building;
   - measures headroom with upward rays;
   - tests navmesh reachability from outside the door (SampleNavmeshPosition and FindPath, with a control path);
   - streams a census JSON.
2. `tools/audit/interior_points.py` does three things:
   - calibrates against stock points on HouseBlock_2F1 and Barracks2, which are classes that have stock points;
   - selects eligible points for each storey;
   - writes a treatment mapgroupproto.xml that changes only the two tenement groups.
3. A later ce-probe-002 run tests the treatment.
